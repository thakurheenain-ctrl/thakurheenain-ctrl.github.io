Meta Ads Strategic Simulation

Finding the Right Buyers Before Scaling Meta Ads

Business: B2B SaaS platform generating 240 leads, 36 SQLs, 18 demos and 4 customers from $12K monthly spend.

Problem: Lead volume was healthy, but the funnel showed a gap between leads, qualified prospects and customers.

Diagnosis: Customer and non-converter feedback revealed a narrower ICP around company size, industry, support-team size and buying needs.

Approach: Refined the ICP and used customer-focused messaging to strengthen qualification while keeping Meta's broad delivery approach.

It started with an ad I couldn't stop seeing.

I first became curious about digital marketing as a social media user. I remember watching a video ad one day and then seeing the same ad again across my social media feeds a few minutes later. I started wondering how that worked. How did the brand know where to show me the ad? Why was I seeing it again so quickly?

What caught my attention was a simple idea: someone has a question, and a business can become the answer. I found that really interesting. Over the next two years, I worked with four companies and built my experience across SEO, content, research, analytics and digital marketing.

I enjoy understanding audiences, improving conversion paths and staying close to the work from planning through execution. I like talking to founders about what they are building, where they want to take it and how marketing can support that journey.

SEO taught me how businesses build visibility over time. Performance marketing showed me how to reach the right audience at the right moment and learn from what happens next.
