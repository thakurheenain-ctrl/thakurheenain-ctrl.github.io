Google Ads Simulation

Finding the gap between lead volume and qualified pipeline

Diagnosing a lead-quality problem and tightening the ad message around the buyer problem that matters.

The Business Challenge

RevFlow was modeled as a B2B SaaS company using Google Search to acquire demand. The starting account produced 200 leads from a $20,000 monthly budget, but only 30 were considered ICP-fit and sales-accepted.

What I Identified

The campaign was being optimized around Demo Form Submitted rather than lead quality. CRM feedback showed only 15% of leads were fit. The existing ad messaging used broad language, while the target market was much narrower.

Execution & Results

I reviewed the starting funnel across clicks, leads, and qualified leads. I developed a revised Google Search ad messaging strategy around opportunity identification rather than broad SaaS benefits.

Primary KPI Shift: Moved from tracking total form submissions to tracking ICP-fit, sales-accepted leads.
