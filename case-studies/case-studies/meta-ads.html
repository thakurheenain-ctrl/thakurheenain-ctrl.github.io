Meta Ads Simulation

Improving lead quality by aligning Meta messaging

Focused on ICP discovery, creative-led qualification, and scaling decisions based on SQLs and revenue.

The Business Challenge

The initial Meta campaign generated lead volume, but the funnel showed a gap between form submissions, sales-qualified leads, demos and customers. The starting scenario used $12,000 in monthly spend and produced 240 leads, 36 SQLs, 18 demos and 4 customers.

Strategy

The strategy was to protect the working campaign while improving the quality of the signals and messages feeding Meta. Customer data showed the strongest fit among 50-150 employee SaaS/technology companies.

Scaling Decision

The simulation did not treat the jump from $12,000 to $20,000 as an automatic next step. The preferred approach was to protect the existing campaign, test additional creative angles, and increase budget gradually once the campaign demonstrated healthy economics.
